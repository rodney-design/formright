# Texas — Contractor Playbook

Internal operating notes, not legal advice — see [README](./README.md).

## Where to file

**Texas Secretary of State** — filed under the Texas Business Organizations
Code (BOC), Title 1 (general) and Title 2, Chapter 22 (Nonprofit
Corporations), using **Form 202 (Certificate of Formation — Nonprofit
Corporation)**. As of a September 2025 policy change, the Secretary of
State accepts formation filings through exactly these channels: **SOSDirect**
(the online portal), **SOSUpload** (electronic PDF upload), in-person
delivery, mail, or courier — no other submission method is accepted.

**SOSDirect vs. SOSUpload** — pick based on the job's urgency:

- **SOSUpload**: upload a completed, executed PDF of Form 202. No account
  setup required beyond basic registration. Typically processed in
  **3–5 business days**.
- **SOSDirect**: requires a **prepaid account** set up in advance — don't
  wait until a same-day job lands to discover the account isn't funded.
  Offers same-day processing for an extra **$25 expedite fee** once the
  account exists.

## Setup quirks

- **No purpose-clause statement is mandated** by Texas the way California
  requires — the generic 501(c)(3) exempt-purpose language in FormRight's
  template is sufficient for the state filing itself.
- **Texas has no general charitable solicitation registration
  requirement** — don't chase one. The only state-level registration
  applies narrowly to law-enforcement, public-safety, and veterans
  organizations soliciting donations, which FormRight doesn't currently
  model as a separate case. If a client's mission is in one of those
  categories, flag it to the admin team rather than assuming Texas is
  registration-free for them specifically.
- When using **SOSUpload**, don't bundle in the instructions PDF, a
  duplicate copy of the document, payment information, or any personal
  identifying information beyond what the form itself requires — the
  Secretary of State's own submission guidelines call this out explicitly,
  and extraneous attachments can slow processing.

## Required attachments / fields

- Entity name
- Registered agent name + Texas street address (P.O. boxes not accepted)
- Names of the initial directors (Texas nonprofit corporations must have a
  board — Form 202 asks for at least the initial slate)
- Management structure statement (Texas nonprofit corporations can be
  managed by members or by a board — the certificate needs to say which)

## Fees & turnaround

- **$25 filing fee**.
- **SOSUpload**: ~3–5 business days. **SOSDirect**: same-day available with
  a prepaid account and the $25 expedite fee. Mail/courier/in-person: budget
  meaningfully longer than either electronic option.

## Common rejection reasons

- **Registered agent is a P.O. box** rather than a physical Texas street
  address — an easy and common mistake to transcribe wrong from the
  worksheet.
- **Management structure not specified** (member-managed vs. board-managed)
  — Form 202 expects an explicit answer, not an assumption.
- **SOSDirect account not funded** before a same-day submission attempt —
  confirm account status before promising same-day turnaround to a client
  or the admin team.
